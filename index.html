VAG Car Data Reader

Drop a .zip containing exported vehicle JSON to read it in a human-readable form.

Everything runs in your browser - no data is uploaded.

Have a VAG vehicle? Use this service to request your data →

Time-series signals

Grouped by data cluster. Click a signal to see its history and chart.

Location track

Plotted locally from the latitude/longitude signals — nothing leaves your browser. The optional street map loads tiles from openstreetmap.org, which reveals the approximate area of the track to that server.

Structured data

Reconstructed from flattened field paths - these are state snapshots, not time-series.
